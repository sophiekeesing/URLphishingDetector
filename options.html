🛡️ URL Shield — settings & privacy

By default URL Shield checks pages only on your device (address heuristics + a small bundled blocklist) and scans a page only when you click the icon. Nothing about your browsing leaves your computer.

The options below are off until you switch them on. Turning one on is your consent for that specific processing (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); you can withdraw it at any time and the on-device checks keep working. Full detail in the privacy policy.

Sends only 4-byte hashed fragments of the page's host and path to safebrowsing.googleapis.com — never the address itself, and never the query string. Google returns a bucket of candidates; the exact match is finished on your device. Requires a free API key.

  1. Enable the Safe Browsing API in Google Cloud Console.
  2. Create an API key under APIs & Services → Credentials.

Sends the hostname only (not the path or query) to two public DNS-over-HTTPS resolvers — cloudflare-dns.com and dns.quad9.net — and compares their answers. These resolvers are independent controllers with their own privacy policies (Cloudflare, Quad9).

Looks up when the domain was first registered, using the public RDAP service each registry runs (the official replacement for WHOIS — free, no account). Phishing domains are usually days old; the sites they imitate are years old, so this catches imitations of brands the built-in list has never heard of.

Sends only the registrable domain (example.com — not the subdomain, path or query) to that TLD's own registry. Covers .com, .net, .org, .info, .xyz, .top, .online, .shop, .uk and others. Many country domains (.lv, .de, .io) publish no RDAP at all — those are reported as unavailable and never count against a site.

Shows a verdict on the toolbar badge for every page without clicking. This needs the tabs permission (the browser will ask). Leaving it off keeps URL Shield strictly scan-on-demand.

Stored with chrome.storage.sync: your settings and API key only. No browsing history, no analytics, no telemetry. A 10-minute in-memory cache of recent verdicts is kept to avoid repeat lookups and is cleared when the service worker stops.
